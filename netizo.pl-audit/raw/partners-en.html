How it works

Four steps. You refer, we take care of the rest.

  1. 01

    You sign up

    You fill in a short form. Within 24 hours you get your partner code and an agreement to sign.

  2. 02

    You refer us

    You give a company you know your code, or pass their contact details on to us. No selling or negotiating needed.

  3. 03

    We do the rest

    We talk to the client, then quote, design and build. You get an email about every order placed with your code.

  4. 04

    You get 15%

    After every client invoice is paid, we transfer 15% of its net value to you within 14 days at the latest.

Your part ends at step two.

Who the program is for

All it takes is talking to business owners. Referrals usually come up on their own, in moments like these:

  • Accounting firm

    A client is starting a business and asks who could build their website. You answer with one contact.

  • Marketing agency

    You run the campaigns, but the client has no website or store to turn that traffic into orders.

  • Designer, photographer, copywriter

    You create logos, photos or copy. A website is usually your client’s next step.

  • Advisor, consultant

    You help companies streamline how they work and see where a system or an app would help.

  • Netizo client

    Someone asks who built your website. From now on, that answer pays off.

  • Someone who knows businesses

    You know business owners from work, your industry or your neighborhood. That’s enough.

Program terms

You get the full agreement to review before you start. Here are the key points.

Commission
15% of the net value of every invoice paid by the referred client.
How long
12 months from the client’s first order: the project, extensions, technical care.
Payout
By bank transfer, no later than 14 days after the client pays the invoice.
Invoicing
You invoice us if you run a business. If you don’t, we pay you under a signed agreement.
Who counts
A new company we haven’t talked to about working together in the last 12 months.
Attribution
Your partner code given by the client, or contact details you pass on with the client’s consent.
Client’s price
The same as without a referral. We pay the commission out of our margin.
Limit
None. Refer as many companies as you like.
