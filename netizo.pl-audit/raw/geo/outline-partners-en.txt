[H2] Common questions
  (p) Can’t find your question? Write to us and we’ll reply within 24 hours.
  (text) Email us
  (button) Who can become a partner?
  (p) Anyone who knows business owners: an accounting firm, a marketing agency, a graphic designer, a consultant, one of our clients or a private person. You don’t need sales experience or any knowledge of websites.
  (button) Do I need to run a business?
  (p) No. Companies invoice us, and with a private person we sign an agreement and pay the commission under it.
  (button) What is the commission based on?
  (p) On the net value of every invoice the referred client pays within 12 months of their first order: for the project, its extensions and technical care. If the client orders a website for PLN 10,000 net, you get PLN 1,500.
  (button) When will I get paid?
  (p) Within 14 days of the day the client pays the invoice. With every payout you get a statement: which order it is for and what amount it is based on.
  (button) How will you know the client came from me?
  (p) The client gives your partner code in the brief or on the first call. You can also pass their contact details to us yourself, with their consent. We confirm every referral to you by email.
  (button) Will the referred client pay more because of it?
  (p) No. We pay the commission out of our margin, so the client gets the same quote as without a referral.
  (button) Do I have to take part in the calls and the quote?
  (p) No. Passing on the contact is enough. We handle the calls, the quote, the design and the launch. If you like, you can join the first meeting.
  (button) What if the referred company has already been in touch with you?
  (p) The commission applies to new clients: companies we haven’t talked to about working together in the last 12 months. If someone has already been in touch, we tell you right after you report the referral.
  (button) Is there a limit on referrals or earnings?
  (p) No. You can refer any number of companies, and you earn a commission on each of them.
  (text) When do I get my commission?
  (text) Within 14 days of the client paying the invoice.
